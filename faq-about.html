About Me!

My name is Monty. I previously worked at St. Honore as the Macaron baker for two years when that was still a position. Despite leaving the proffession I still love baking so when I left I was excited to have more time to spend on my passion project - The Bug Bakery! I was heavily inspired by Tucca and Berties Bug Bundts and found myself spending any free time I had thinking either about how I would pastry a bug or bug a pastry!
